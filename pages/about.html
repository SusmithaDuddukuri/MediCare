About MediCare Pro

MediCare Pro is a comprehensive Hospital Management System designed for modern healthcare facilities. It provides end-to-end digital solutions for patient care, appointments, electronic health records, pharmacy inventory, billing, and advanced analytics.

Built with real-world hospital workflows in mind, the system supports multi-specialty clinics, 24/7 emergency operations, integrated pharmacy, and role-based access for doctors, nurses, receptionists, and administrators.

Key Features

  • Smart Appointment Scheduling with conflict detection
  • Complete Patient EHR with history and prescriptions
  • Pharmacy Inventory with stock alerts and expiry tracking
  • Automated Billing and Insurance claim support
  • Real-time Dashboards and Custom Reports
  • Role-based Security and Audit Logs

Trusted by hospitals across India for reliable, scalable healthcare operations.
